# Cursor shipped Projects. Staffing agencies still take the cut.

Cursor’s Projects launch is the right *shape*: one coordinator, a persistent thread, subagents that don’t forget the plan. I ran the same shape years before it had a product name. I’m an AI & systems engineer, Autodev Team, Grok Bot Chief of Staff. Ex-FAANG. Pending US patents in AI. I run **4** Autodev Grok Bots — CoS, Research, Demand, Offer. CoS is the front door. It routes. It doesn’t freelance.

Here’s the part the launch thread won’t say: a nicer coordinator does not kill the staffing agency. It just gives the agency a better IDE to mark up.

## AI mostly sucks

I say this as someone whose entire working life is downstream of models. Most “AI transformation” is a slide. Most agent demos are a staffing brochure with a progress bar. The only people who consistently get leverage are engineers who already knew how to ship — architecture, contracts, taste, the willingness to throw work away.

If you’re not that, Projects will help you open more chats. It will not make you a lab.

That’s not a dunk on Cursor. It’s a dunk on the industry that sold “agents replace teams” to the same buyers who used to buy “we’ll staff you a pod in 48 hours.” Different logo. Same cut.

## The middleman is the product

Staffing agencies exist to sit between the company that needs software and the people who can write it. They take the spread. They rename “engineer” as “resource.” They sell hope and a bench.

SaaS agent platforms are learning the same trick: sell *seats*, not *ships*. You’re not hiring capacity. You’re renting a coordinator and hoping the subagents hallucinate a roadmap you can show your VP.

I built Autodev to delete that layer. Onshore, hybrid, human–AI staff-aug. You buy elite hours and a platform that actually runs the SDLC — not a prompt window with a price list. No upfront theater. Hours and scope agreed ahead. Invoice at Autodev UAT for that scope. Change orders before start. Not pay-when-happy. Not free-forever discovery. Not open-ended T&M.

If that sounds less magical than “40 agents run your company,” good. Magic is how agencies price.

## Coordinator ≠ Chief of Staff

Projects: one persistent thread, a coordinator, subagents, memory that syncs.

Autodev: four specialized Grok Bots, one CoS, owner-approved handoffs. A receipt is not proof they read it. The human still owns architecture. The bots execute inside contracts.

Same geometry. Different contract. One is a product seat. One is capacity you couldn’t staff through a middleman without lighting the budget on fire.

I don’t need Cursor to be wrong for Autodev to be right. I need buyers to stop confusing *an IDE that delegates* with *a team that ships*.

## Commoditize the expensive part

The goal is not a prettier agency. The goal is to make frontier-quality build capacity cheap enough that the agency markup looks stupid. Commoditize the harness. Keep the scarce thing scarce: engineers with judgment.

If AI ever gets good enough that this essay is embarrassing, I’ll take it. Until then, most of it sucks, engineers get the upside, and anyone charging you a staffing spread to wrap a coordinator should be out of the deal.
