# ChatGPT for Finance is a seat. The staffing firms will mark it up.

OpenAI’s post is the whole brief: ChatGPT for Financial Services. Tailored Work experience. Built-in financial data. GPT-6 Astra reasoning. Teams can “develop research, build financial models, and create customized client materials.”

That’s a **seat**. A nicer prompt box with Bloomberg-flavored garnish. I didn’t need the 81-second trailer. The caption already sold the dream.

I’m an AI & systems engineer, Autodev Team, Grok Bot Chief of Staff. Ex-FAANG. Pending US patents in AI. I run **4** Autodev Grok Bots. CoS is the front door. It routes. It doesn’t freelance.

Here’s the riff the launch won’t print: every staffing firm on the street will wrap this and send you a “quant pod” deck by Friday. Same cut. New logo.

## AI mostly sucks

I say this as someone whose paycheck is downstream of models. Most “AI for finance” is a slide with a compliance footnote. The people who get leverage already knew how to build a model, argue a number, and throw a bad sheet away.

If you’re not that, ChatGPT Work will help you generate more decks. It will not make you a lab. It will not make your bank stop paying a middleman to babysit the tool.

That’s not a dunk on OpenAI. It’s a dunk on the industry that sells *access* as *capacity*.

## The middleman loves a new SKU

Staffing agencies exist to sit between the firm that needs work and the people who can do it. They take the spread. They rename “engineer” as “resource.” They sell hope and a bench.

Now they get a new SKU: “ChatGPT for Financial Services, staffed.” You’ll buy seats *and* a markup. SaaS sold the chair. The agency sold the sitting.

I built Autodev to delete that layer. Onshore, hybrid, human–AI staff-aug. You buy elite hours and a platform that runs the SDLC — not a Work tab with a price list. Hours and scope agreed ahead. Invoice at Autodev UAT. Change orders before start. Not pay-when-happy. Not open-ended T&M.

If that sounds less magical than “Astra builds your model,” good. Magic is how agencies price.

## Coordinator ≠ team that ships

OpenAI: one Work surface, data connectors, reasoning, client-ready exports.

Autodev: four specialized Grok Bots, one CoS, owner-approved handoffs. A receipt is not proof they read it. The human still owns the architecture. The bots execute inside contracts.

Same geometry as every “always-on agent” launch this month. Different contract. One is a product seat. One is capacity you couldn’t staff through a middleman without lighting the budget on fire.

I don’t need ChatGPT for Finance to be wrong. I need buyers to stop confusing a tailored chat with a team that ships.

## Commoditize the expensive part

The goal is not a prettier finance plugin. The goal is to make frontier-quality build capacity cheap enough that the agency markup looks stupid. Commoditize the harness. Keep the scarce thing scarce: engineers with judgment.

Until the models embarrass this essay, most of it sucks, engineers get the upside, and anyone charging you a staffing spread to wrap a Work tab should be out of the deal.
