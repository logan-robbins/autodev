# Discovery script — Autodev Team

**Goal:** Book only buyers who need **onshore hybrid staff-aug capacity that ships**. Kill DIY-platform tire-kickers before they waste Logan's calendar (form Q0 does the same gate).  
**Length:** 30–45 min.  
**Updated:** 2026-09-11 (footnote placement: short high / full low — match site)  
**Aligns:** CHARTER props · `hyperagent-map.md` · `hyperagent-gtm-teardown.md` · `icp-v0.md` · Demand M1–M5 → `leadgen-fields.md`

**White-space:** staff-aug economics (fractional vs FTE, quality held) — never “our agents are smarter.”

---

## Pre-call (2 min)

- Read Lead Gen answers (esp. Q0 + trigger).  
- Lead with delivery / staff-aug outcome, not console tour.  
- If Q0 = platform/seats for their team → soft DQ email; do **not** book.

---

## Open (2 min)

> Thanks for the time. Quick frame: Autodev Team is **100% onshore hybrid human–AI staff-aug** — operators who run Autodev and deliver software into your GitHub/cloud at fractional cost vs filling senior FTEs, without quality sacrifice. We are **not** SaaS/PaaS you configure. If you want seats for your team to run agents, we're the wrong buy — I'll say so in the first five minutes.

Confirm gate heard. Agenda: problem → why now → staff-aug fit → how we deliver → economics → next step.

---

## Gate block (5 min) — mandatory

**Q1 — Job to be done**  
"What has to be true in 90 days that isn't true today — shipped product, platform rebuild, backlog burn-down, something else?"

**Q2 — Platform vs staff-aug capacity**  
"Are you evaluating tools for *your* eng team to run agents, or buying *fractional delivery capacity* because headcount/speed won't get there?"

| Answer | Move |
|---|---|
| Capacity / ship / freeze + slip / staff-aug | Continue |
| DIY platform / HyperAgent-class / "we want seats" | Soft DQ |
| Unsure | Probe: who reviews agent output? who owns merge? "We'll figure it out" → DIY risk → DQ or educate once |

**Q3 — DIY / hiring history**  
"What have you tried — Cursor, Claude Code, Devin-class, HyperAgent/agent PaaS — and where's the hiring freeze or senior gap that makes FTE hard?"

Kill early:
- Primary need = marketing/ops/Slack agents  
- Budget = SaaS seats only, no services  
- Expects no-humans autonomous delivery  
- Won't discuss GitHub/cloud delivery or security review

---

## Situation (8–10 min)

Ask only what Lead Gen missed:

1. **Roadmap pressure** — What's slipping? Who feels it?  
2. **Headcount economics** — Freeze, stale senior reqs, attrition → fractional vs FTE comparison  
3. **Security** — Block on cloud agent SaaS? Clean room / air-gap / onshore matter?  
4. **Ownership** — Economic buyer (CTO/VP Eng)? Champion? Killer (security/procurement)?  
5. **Success metric** — Accepted features / milestone — not “agents running.”

---

## Pitch bridge (5 min) — staff-aug, not feature dump

Only after gate passes:

> You're still hiring expensive engineers — ours are onshore, hybrid with Autodev (humans direct; harnesses multiply). **You still decide** — operators + contracts + review gates; nothing “ships itself.” Quality held vs senior FTE; cost is fractional staff-aug, not a credit meter. Verified software + evidence into your cloud.

**Named capacity SKU (steal HyperAgent motion, flip category):** talk one **Pillar/Pod outcome** (e.g. Frontend / Research / Infra), not agent seat names — same craft as their Funnel/Signal posts, our product is staff-aug delivery.

**Before/after (format only — no invented $):** freeze / slip / stalled DIY → fractional onshore bench shipping with evidence (demo board/fleet). Never borrow Appell/HelloPackage metrics.

Touch once each: **100% onshore** · **hybrid staff-aug** · **fractional vs FTE** · **human still decides** · **capacity = staffing decision** (not model leaderboard) · itemized quote ($100/agent/mo + hours + AI subs + tokens — our flip of their runtime+$ cards) · clean room if security came up.  
**Never** “better agents” / “employable agents” / connector wars / Marketplace / credits / Founding 500. Name HyperAgent only if *they* did: wrong category if job = software delivery.

Demo optional (3 min `/demo`) after problem clarity.

---

## Economics & next step (5 min)

- **Payment prop (say once, high):** No upfront payment. Pay at UAT milestones.*  
  **Expand only if asked / in one-pager send:**  
  * Hours and scope agreed ahead · invoice at Autodev UAT for that scope · change orders before start.
  * (hygiene) Invoices at Autodev-defined UAT acceptance for that scope. Hours and scope are agreed ahead of time. Change orders quoted before start. ≠ pay-when-happy, free forever discovery, open-ended T&M / verbal-only, or “no pay until prod” unless SOW final UAT is cutover.
- Frame quote as **staffing decision** (staff-aug vs open FTE / freeze) — not model leaderboard, not HyperAgent credits / Marketplace / Founding 500.  
- Itemized before work: staff hours · $100/agent/mo · AI subs · tokens.  
- Ladder: **named scoped Pillar** (one job outcome) → retainer fractional capacity. No self-serve.  
- Ask: timeline, budget band, decision process.  
- **Close:** "If this is staff-aug capacity you need, I'll send the one-pager + a **named Pillar outline** (one outcome, contracts first). If you mainly need a platform to configure, I'll point you there and we stop."
