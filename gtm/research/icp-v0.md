# ICP v0 — Autodev Team

**Model lock:** 100% onshore hybrid human–AI staff-aug. Buyer hires stealth top-lab engineers operating Autodev at fractional rates without quality sacrifice — not SaaS/PaaS.  
**Primary channel:** LinkedIn Company Page + organic + Lead Gen.  
**Updated:** 2026-09-10 (Logan prop lock)

---

## Who buys

**Economic buyer:** VP Eng / CTO / Head of Product who owns roadmap velocity and headcount cost.  
**Champion:** Eng manager / Staff+ who has tried DIY agents (Cursor/Claude Code/HyperAgent-class) and hit review/ops ceiling.  
**Blocker:** Security / procurement (we win with clean room, onshore, itemized quote).

**Not ICP:** Solo founders wanting a $20/mo agent seat; marketing ops buying Slack bots; teams whose only need is internal workflow automation (that's HyperAgent).

## Company stages (priority order)

| Tier | Stage | Why they buy | Deal shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Series B–D product companies, 50–400 eng-adjacent | Roadmap slip + senior hiring freeze; need parallel delivery without 6-month ramp | Scoped Pillar/Pod → retainer |
| **B** | Late seed / Series A with funded ambition | Must ship v1/v2 fast; can't staff stealth talent | Fixed-scope engagement |
| **C** | Mid-market / enterprise product orgs (select) | Backlog that never moves; air-gap / clean-room requirement | Pilot in their cloud → embedded capacity |
| **D** | Agencies / "AI transformation" consultancies | Want white-label delivery capacity | Partner motion later — not Day-0 |

**Geography v0:** US-primary (onshore claim); English-speaking product orgs.  
**Verticals v0 (hypothesis):** B2B SaaS, developer tools, fintech/infra, AI-native products rebuilding core systems — *not* marketing-site factories.

## Disqualify fast

- Wants self-serve Autodev license only, no operators  
- Primary need = CRM/marketing/ops agents  
- Budget only for SaaS seats (<~ mid four figures/mo) with no services  
- Expects fully autonomous "no humans" delivery  
- Won't allow GitHub/cloud delivery path or security review
