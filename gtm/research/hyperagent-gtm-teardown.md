### Hook formulas (steal structure)

1. **Imperative + loop** — “Your ads need to be running in a self-improving loop.” then bulleted agent steps → Marketplace CTA.  
2. **Identity tribe** — “Agent-pilled sales/GTM/data teams…” (in-group language).  
3. **Before/after ops math** — hours → minutes; buildings 50→200; $ quotes in two weeks.  
4. **Job title for the agent** — Signal / Funnel / Vera / Redline / Inbound (productized roles, not vague “AI”).  
5. **Human still decides** — “You still make the call… Nothing sends without a human reviewing it first.” (reduces autonomy fear).  
6. **Outcome trust bar** — “employable” = works when you’re not watching + team handoffs + learns on the job.
